# Board Card

**Keep this open in a second tab while playing. Nothing here is new — it's the
system stripped to what I can actually read mid-game.**

---

## Every single move, before I touch a piece

```
1. CHECKS      — every check I have
2. CAPTURES    — every capture I have
3. THREATS     — anything undefended? any fork?
```

**If a quiet move feels obviously right — that is the trigger to scan, not to
skip it.** All seven documented blunders were quiet moves that felt fine.

---

## After they move

```
What did that move ATTACK?
What did it stop DEFENDING?
Do they have a check or capture next?
Was it UNUSUAL?  →  if yes, STOP the script, run Scan A
```

---

## Loose pieces

**Mine** — what's about to get taken?
**Theirs** — that's where my tactic is.

---

## Before ANY capture on a contested square

Count attackers. Count defenders. Count the order.
Attackers ≤ defenders? Usually don't take.

---

## When Scan A is genuinely empty

> **What is the position asking me, and what is my laziest piece?**

Improve the worst piece. A rook shuffling is not a plan.

---

## Hard rules

- **Castle by move 10.** Both colours. No exceptions I've ever justified.
- **Recapture near my king with a PIECE, not a pawn.**
- **When winning big: TRADE.** Simplify. Don't keep it complicated.
- **Something of mine attacked?** Before saving it — is there something BIGGER?
- **Never resign.** Endgame is my best phase. Opponents crack. Game 8 ended in
  mate on move 67.

---

## State check, before I click "Play"

```
Am I fresh?                    → play
Just finished League/Fortnite? → DON'T play rated. Puzzles only.
Late at night?                 → puzzles only
Tilted from a loss?            → one more game MAX, then stop
```
